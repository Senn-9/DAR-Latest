# EXHIBIT C: Training and Support Details

## 1. TRAINING SCOPE

1.1. Duration: The Academic Partner and its interns may provide up to one (1) week of training and support to the Host Industry's personnel on the use and implementation of the Technology, as detailed in this Exhibit.

1.2. Topics to be Covered
- System navigation and role-based access (Admin, Division Head, BAC, PARPO, Budget, Supply, Accounting, Cash, End Users)
- Purchase Request creation, submission, and tracking
- Canvassing workflow and quotation submission
- BAC resolution and Abstract of Awards
- Purchase Order generation and delivery receipt processing
- Disbursement Voucher / Payment workflow and status tracking
- Budget allocation, utilization monitoring, and reports
- File attachments, PDF export, and document retrieval (Supabase Storage)
- Dashboard usage, analytics, and basic troubleshooting

1.3. Delivery Method
Training shall be conducted either:
- On-site at the Host's premises; or
- Virtually via an online conferencing platform (Zoom, Microsoft Teams, or similar).

## 2. POST-TRAINING SUPPORT

2.1. Support Period
- A thirty (30) calendar day period of remote support via email and/or chat for technical inquiries and assistance shall be provided following the training session.

2.2. Support Scope
- Clarification of workflows and role behaviors
- Assistance with user account issues and access permissions
- Help with viewing reports, PDFs, and attachments
- Troubleshooting common UI issues and guidance on best practices
- Minor configuration guidance (e.g., division setup, role assignment)

2.3. Additional Support
- Any further technical support or services beyond the initial training period shall be compensated accordingly to the interns or graduates involved, at rates mutually agreed upon in a separate written agreement or service contract.

## 3. RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1. Developer / Trainer Responsibilities
- Provide qualified trainers familiar with the DAR Procurement and Monitoring System.
- Deliver the agreed training content and materials (electronic slides, sample procedures).
- Respond to post-training inquiries during the 30-day support period.

3.2. Host Responsibilities
- Ensure attendance of relevant personnel.
- Provide necessary infrastructure for on-site training (computers, internet access) or coordinate virtual meeting logistics.
- Designate point(s) of contact for scheduling and follow-up.

## 4. DELIVERY OF TRAINING MATERIALS

- Training materials (slide deck and concise quick-start guide) will be provided in PDF format.
- A copy of the user manual (`docs/USER_MANUAL.md`) will be delivered electronically.

## 5. AMENDMENTS

Any changes to the training scope, schedule, or support terms shall require mutual written agreement.
