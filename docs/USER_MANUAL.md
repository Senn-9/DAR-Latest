# USER MANUAL - DAR Procurement and Monitoring System

**Easy-to-follow guide for all staff and administrators**

This manual explains how to use the DAR Procurement and Monitoring System in simple terms. Even if you are not familiar with computers, you can follow these step-by-step instructions.

## 1. Getting Started

1. **Open the system:** Ask your IT administrator for the website link. Copy and paste it into your browser.
2. **Log in:** Enter your username and password. Click the login button.
3. **Use any modern web browser:** Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari all work fine.
4. **Do not have an account yet?** Ask your Division Head or the system administrator to create one for you.

## 2. What Role Do You Have?

Your role determines what you can do in the system. Here are the main roles:

- **Administrator (Admin):** You manage the entire system. You can create accounts, assign roles, and view reports. You do not create purchase requests.
- **Division Head:** You manage purchase requests for your division and approve them before they go to the next stage.
- **End User / Staff:** You create and submit purchase requests when your office needs to buy something.
- **BAC (Committee):** You review bids from suppliers and choose the best one.
- **PARPO:** You approve the purchase after the BAC review.
- **Budget Officer:** You watch the budget and make sure money is not overspent.
- **Supply Officer:** You track deliveries and make sure items arrive correctly.
- **Accounting / Cash Officer:** You handle payments and final approvals.

If you are not sure which role you have, ask the system administrator.

## 3. How to Log In

1. Open the website link your IT administrator gave you.
2. Type your username (the name the administrator assigned to you).
3. Type your password.
4. Click the "Log In" or "Sign In" button.
5. Wait for the page to load. You should see the main menu or dashboard.

**Forgot your password?** Ask the system administrator to reset it for you.

## 4. Creating a Purchase Request (For Staff and End Users)

Use this section if your job is to create a request for something your office needs.

**Step-by-Step:**

1. Find and click "Procurement" in the main menu.
2. Click on "Purchase Request" section.
3. Click the "New Purchase Request" button (it may say "+ New PR" or similar).
4. Fill in the top section with information like:
   - What department is requesting this?
   - What is the money for? (Fund type)
   - What office or section are you in?
   - What is today's date?
5. Add the items you want to buy:
   - Click "+ Add Item" for each thing you need.
   - Enter how many you need (Quantity).
   - Enter the price per item (Unit Cost).
   - Describe what you need in simple words.
6. Review everything and make sure it is correct.
7. Click "Save" to keep your work without sending it yet. You can come back to it later.
8. When you are ready to send it for approval, click "Submit" button.

**Important:** Only your own draft requests show the Submit button. Once you submit, you cannot edit it again.

## 5. Reviewing and Approving a Purchase Request (For Division Head and Admin)

Use this section if your job is to check and approve purchase requests.

**Step-by-Step:**

1. Click "Procurement" in the main menu.
2. Click "All Purchase Requests" or "Purchase Requests" to see the full list.
3. Look for the request you need to review:
   - You can search by the request number.
   - You can search by the office section.
   - You can scroll down to find it.
4. Click on the request to open and read it.
5. Check all the information to make sure it looks correct.
6. If you approve it, click the "Approve" or "Process" button.
7. Choose what happens next (the system will guide you).
8. Click "Save" or "Confirm" to move the request forward.

**Tip:** Keep notes about what you approved and why. The system saves this information.

## 6. Asking Suppliers for Prices (Canvassing)

Use this section if you are the BAC (Bids and Awards Committee) member.

**Step-by-Step:**

1. Click "Procurement" in the main menu.
2. Find the section for Canvassing or "Get Quotations."
3. Create a new canvassing record by clicking "+ New" or "+ Canvassing."
4. Choose the approved purchase request you want to get prices for.
5. List the suppliers you want to ask for prices.
6. Upload or attach the suppliers' price quotes (PDFs or Excel files), if attachment upload is enabled by your administrator.
7. Compare the quotes to find the best price and quality.
8. Mark the winning supplier (the one with the best offer).
9. Click "Save" when done.

**After Canvassing:**

1. Prepare a BAC Resolution to explain why you chose this supplier.
2. Create an "Abstract of Awards" which is the official record of your choice.

## 7. Creating a Purchase Order (PO) and Tracking Delivery

**Creating a Purchase Order:**

1. Click "Procurement" in the main menu.
2. Find "Purchase Order" or "PO" section.
3. Click "+ New Purchase Order."
4. Choose the approved purchase request.
5. Review the supplier information and items.
6. Save the Purchase Order.
7. You can print it or send it to the supplier.

**Tracking Delivery:**

1. Click "Procurement" in the main menu.
2. Find "Delivery" or "Deliveries" section.
3. Click "+ New Delivery" when items arrive.
4. Enter the delivery date and details (how many items came, in what condition).
5. Attach the delivery receipt or inspection document if your deployment has storage configured.
6. Click "Save" to record that the items arrived.

## 8. Processing Payments

Use this section if you handle payments.

**Step-by-Step:**

1. Click "Procurement" in the main menu.
2. Find "Payment" or "Disbursement Voucher" section.
3. Click "+ New Payment" or "+ New DV."
4. Fill in the payment details (supplier name, amount, reason for payment).
5. Click "Save" to create the payment request.
6. The payment will go through several checks:
   - PARPO reviews it.
   - Accounting verifies the amount and paperwork.
   - Cash officer approves the final payment.
7. Check the status to see where the payment is in the approval process.

**Checking Payment Status:**

1. Go to the Payment section.
2. Find your payment by searching or scrolling.
3. Click on it to see all the approval steps and dates.

## 9. Checking and Managing the Budget (For Budget Officers)

**Viewing the Budget:**

1. Click "Budget" in the main menu.
2. Choose the division or year you want to check.
3. Look at how much money was planned (budget) and how much was actually spent.
4. Check the percentage used to see if you are within budget.

**Creating or Changing a Budget:**

1. Click "Budget" in the main menu.
2. Click "+ New Budget" or find the budget to edit.
3. Enter the total amount for the division and year.
4. Click "Save."

## 10. Managing User Accounts (For Administrators)

**Adding a New User:**

1. Click "User Management" in the main menu.
2. Click "+ New User" or "Add User."
3. Enter the person's username (their login name).
4. Enter their email address.
5. Choose their role from the dropdown list (Admin, Division Head, End User, etc.).
6. Choose their division (what department they belong to).
7. Click "Save."
8. The system will send them their password or ask them to create one.

**Editing a User:**

1. Find the user in the list.
2. Click on their name or row.
3. Change any information you need to update.
4. Click "Save."

**Deleting a User:**

1. Find the user in the list.
2. Click the Delete button (usually a trash icon).
3. Confirm you want to delete them.

**Resetting a Password:**

1. Find the user in the list.
2. Click "Reset Password" or similar button.
3. Send them the new temporary password.

## 11. Uploading Files and Printing Reports

**Uploading Files (if enabled in your deployment):**

In DAR-Latest, attachment upload is available in canvassing and related workflow steps when Supabase Storage is configured by your administrator.

1. Look for a button that says "Upload," "Attach," or "+."
2. Click it and choose the file from your computer.
3. Wait for it to finish uploading (you will see a confirmation).
4. Click "Done" or "Save."

**Supported File Types:** PDF, Word (DOCX), Excel (XLSX, XLS), images (JPG, PNG).

**Printing or Exporting to PDF:**

1. Find the document, report, or form you want to print.
2. Look for a "Print," "Export," or "Download" button.
3. Click it to save the file to your computer or send it to a printer.
4. If a dialog appears, choose your printer and settings.
5. Click "Print."

## 12. Troubleshooting - Solutions to Common Problems

### Problem: I Cannot Log In

**What to do:**
- Make sure you are typing your username and password correctly. They are case-sensitive (capital letters matter).
- Ask the administrator to confirm your account was created.
- Try refreshing the page (press F5 on your keyboard).
- Try using a different web browser.
- If you forgot your password, click "Forgot Password" on the login page or ask the administrator to reset it.

### Problem: I Do Not See the Button or Menu I Need

**What to do:**
- Your role may not have permission to use that feature.
- Log out and back in to refresh your permissions.
- Ask the administrator to check your role and division assignment.
- You may be looking in the wrong section; check the main menu on the left side.

### Problem: My Purchase Request Will Not Save

**What to do:**
- Fill in all the required fields (marked with * or "Required").
- Check that numbers are in the quantity and price fields (no letters).
- Make sure your internet connection is working.
- Refresh the page and try again.
- If the problem continues, contact the administrator with the error message you see.

### Problem: I Cannot Upload a File

**What to do:**
- Confirm with your administrator that attachment upload is enabled in your deployment.
- Check the file type. Allowed types are: PDF, Word, Excel, images.
- Make sure the file size is not too large.
- Give the file a simple name without special characters (like !, @, #).
- Try a different web browser.
- Try uploading the file again.

### Problem: I Cannot Print or Export to PDF

**What to do:**
- Wait a few seconds; the PDF might be loading.
- Check that your browser allows downloads and pop-ups (check the address bar).
- Try again after refreshing the page.
- Make sure you have enough space on your computer to save the file.

### Problem: The System Is Moving Very Slowly or Not Loading

**What to do:**
- Wait 30 seconds for the page to fully load.
- Refresh the page (press F5 on your keyboard).
- Close other browser tabs to save memory.
- Restart your browser completely.
- Check your internet connection; try opening another website to confirm.
- Contact the administrator if the problem continues.

### Problem: I See a Red Error Message

**What to do:**
- Read the error message carefully; it may tell you what went wrong.
- Take a screenshot of the error message and send it to the administrator.
- Do not close the browser window yet; the administrator may want to see it.
- Try to perform the action again after a few minutes.

### If You Cannot Fix It

1. Write down what you were trying to do.
2. Write down the exact error message you see.
3. Take a screenshot if possible.
4. Contact the system administrator or help desk with this information.
5. Be patient; they will help you solve the problem.

## 13. How to Use This Manual

- Read the section that matches your role and task.
- Follow the numbered steps in order.
- Use the troubleshooting section when you encounter errors.
- If you have a question not answered here, ask your administrator or colleague for help.

## 14. Quick Tips for Everyone

- **Save your work often.** Do not lose your changes by closing the browser without saving.
- **Take notes.** Write down request numbers and dates for your records.
- **Ask questions.** It is better to ask than to make a mistake.
- **Use the latest browser.** Update Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari to the newest version.
- **Contact the administrator for help.** Do not guess at what to do if something seems wrong.
